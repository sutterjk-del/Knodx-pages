What if the right people and the right AI capabilities were already working together?

Product & operations

Engineers, operators and their AIs could share requirements, inspect evidence, test and resolve blockers.

These examples illustrate the intended collaboration model. Individual integrations and automated workflows require validation, access and applicable approval before use.

Trust by design

Connected does not mean unrestricted.

People should remain in control of their data, their AIs and their decisions. Our design prioritizes scoped access, attributable actions, revocation, evidence and human approval when needed.

Mission Control and the broader multi-provider intelligence network are in development. This page describes our direction, not a claim that every provider, feature or automated collaboration is currently available.
